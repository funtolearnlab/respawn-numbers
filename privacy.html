Respawn Numbers · Last updated: 16 October 2026

About this site

Respawn Numbers is an independent editorial project about game-statistics methods, transparent calculations, and illustrative gaming datasets. This notice describes the website’s current publishing setup and the information involved when you read or interact with it. It covers this project; linked services have their own notices.

Hosting and technical information

GitHub Pages serves this static website. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, whether or not a visitor is signed in. Its privacy statement describes its service practices. The site’s editors have not added a separate visitor database, advertising tag, or third-party analytics service to these pages.

The editors have not added separate analytics or advertising tags, a payment processor, or a contact form. This does not mean that visiting the site produces no data: its hosting platform still delivers the pages and may process technical information.

Browser-only calculators

Calculator inputs are processed on your device by JavaScript. They are not sent to the editors or stored by this site. The tools do not connect to game accounts or ask for player identifiers.

Information you choose to provide

The website itself has no account signup, checkout, contact form, or built-in comment submission. If you choose to visit its source repository, open an issue, or contribute through GitHub, those are separate platform interactions. Your GitHub username and anything you post in a public repository may be visible to others. Do not include private player data in contributions.

Cookies, choices, and external links

You can review your browser’s cookie controls and any privacy choices offered by GitHub Pages. Blocking cookies may affect some platform features. The provider’s GitHub General Privacy Statement explains its handling, sharing, retention, and request routes; this site does not set the provider’s operational retention periods.

Links to game publishers, stores, and other resources lead to separate services with their own privacy practices. If embedded third-party media is present, its provider may receive technical data when it loads. Check the destination notice before sharing information.

Contact and changes

No direct public contact channel for this editorial project is currently listed. For data processed by the hosting provider, use the privacy controls and request routes in its linked policy. Those provider routes concern the provider’s processing and are not a substitute for contacting the project about data it independently handles.

The date on this page records its latest revision. This notice will need updating if the site adds a contact route, forms, analytics, paid features, or another use of reader information.
